# Code of conduct

This is a small project used by a lot of people who mostly want their game to look the way they
like. Most of them arrive here because something broke. Treat everyone the way you would want to
be treated on the day it is your game that stopped working.

## Expected

- Patience with people new to modding, to GitHub or to English. A clumsy report is still a report.
- Arguing with code, decisions and evidence, not with the person who brought them.
- Reports that say what you did, what happened and what you expected, rather than how you feel
  about it.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexual remarks about anyone.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or bringing in a crowd from somewhere else to pile on.
- Cheats, account trading, or anything that touches other players' accounts. This project changes
  how the game looks on your own machine and nothing more.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments in this repository, and the project's
channel on the D2PFX Discord when you are speaking there about this project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer, TheFleece, privately: **hello@dota2modmanager.com**, or a direct
message to **Fleece** on the [D2PFX Discord](https://discord.gg/PBvG8D9MxT). The address is there
because a Discord message means joining a server first, and somebody reporting how they were
treated should not have to stand in the room to do it. Reports are read within 72 hours and kept
between you and the maintainer.

## What happens

The maintainer decides and says what was decided: a warning, a removed comment, or a block from
the repository, depending on what happened and whether it happened before.
