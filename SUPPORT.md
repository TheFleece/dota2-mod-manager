# Getting help

**Something does not work.** Open an issue with the bug form and attach the diagnostic archive:
Settings, Diagnostics, Export report. It holds the app log, the load order and what the app knows
about your game folder, and it turns most reports into a short fix. Read it before attaching it:
it names your game folder and, on Windows, your account name.

**A question about using the app.** The [guides](https://dota2modmanager.com/docs/) cover
installing, languages, your own mods, the cosmetics feature and whether any of it is safe. If they
do not answer it, ask in `#mod-manager` on the [D2PFX Discord](https://discord.gg/PBvG8D9MxT) or
open an issue.

**A mod looks wrong in the game.** The mods themselves come from the D2PFX catalog, and every card
in the app names the mod's author. A broken model or a wrong texture is theirs to fix; an app that
installs the mod into the wrong place is ours.

**A security problem.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never a public issue.

How fast you can expect an answer is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#response-times).
